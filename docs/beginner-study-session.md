## Observed Problems

For each problem, record the screen/control, the action, the observed difficulty and its consequence. Separate observation from proposed explanation. Include application errors and unrelated interruptions; never silently exclude them.

## Debrief

What was confusing? What helped explain the result? What did the participant expect to happen differently? Only after scoring, explain misconceptions and answer questions. Do not rewrite the earlier record using what the participant learned during the debrief.
